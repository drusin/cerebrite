Stable heading identity: the temporary-mapping demo

Question: when a link points at a heading and that heading is later renamed or moved, can Cerebrite keep the link resolving correctly without writing any extra metadata into the markdown body (clean markdown, ADR-0003)? The leading idea: a temporary in-app mapping (a redirect table, living only in the derived index) bridges the gap immediately after the edit, and a background cleanup job rewrites the referencing files to point at the new heading directly — after which the mapping entry is disposable.
